### 👥 Users
- [[User Personas]] — Target profiles: chronic illness patients, frequent travelers, parents/caregivers, and independent clinics.
- [[User Pain Points]] — Fragmented portal logins, lost paper charts, repeat diagnostic tests, and slow records requests.

### 📋 Requirements
- [[Functional Requirements]] — Core capabilities: document ingestion, metadata tagging, search, export, and secure transmission.
- [[Non-Functional Requirements]] — Performance latency, offline accessibility, local encryption standards, and cross-platform compatibility.

### ✨ Features
- [[Patient Onboarding]] — Pre-signin clinical value carousel, first-launch persistent storage guard, and replay tour.
- [[Digital Medical Records]] — Structured digitization of lab tests, prescriptions, doctor notes, and immunizations.
- [[Record Sharing]] — Time-limited, revocable links and QR-code mediated record sharing.
- [[Record Transfer]] — Direct peer-to-peer or standardized export (PDF, JSON/FHIR) to receiving providers.
