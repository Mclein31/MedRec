## 🎯 Purpose of This Log

As a solo developer, there is no team to conduct retrospective meetings with. This log acts as my personal feedback loop:
- Capturing mistakes early so they are not repeated in future phases.
- Recognizing incorrect technical or product assumptions.
- Documenting why certain architectural pivots had to happen.

---

## 📝 Lesson Template

When a non-trivial realization occurs or a mistake is made, document it using this format:

```markdown
### [Short, Memorable Title of Lesson]
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Context**: What was I trying to accomplish, and what situation triggered this realization?
- **The Mistake or Misconception**: What assumption turned out to be wrong?
- **The Lesson**: What is the core truth or rule of thumb discovered?
- **Impact / Action Taken**: How does this change my architecture, code, research plan, or workflow going forward?
- **Related**: [[Related Note]]
```
