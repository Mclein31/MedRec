### Landscape Research & Domain De-risking (2026-08-24)
- [x] **Initial Landscape Research Deliverables**: Completed comprehensive secondary landscape research (competitive analysis, regulatory/standards landscape, academic literature review). Created [[Competitive Landscape]], [[Regulatory Landscape]], and [[Academic Literature Summary]] notes.
- [x] **Competitive Analysis**: Analyzed market solutions (Apple Health Records, Epic MyChart, Guava Health, Fasten Health, PicnicHealth) and post-mortem failure causes of Microsoft HealthVault and Google Health v1 in [[Competitive Landscape]].
- [x] **Regulatory & Legal Landscape**: Analyzed ONC 21st Century Cures Act, FHIR R4 mandates, HIPAA covered-entity vs consumer app boundaries, FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, and state privacy laws (WA MHMDA, CCPA) in [[Regulatory Landscape]].
