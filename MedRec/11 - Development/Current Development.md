## 🔬 Active Streams of Work

### 1. Problem Exploration & Literature Review
- **Focus**: Reviewing existing literature on electronic health record (EHR) fragmentation, personal health records (PHRs), and patient portal abandonment.
- **Related Notes**: [[Literature Review]], [[Research Questions]], [[Research Themes]]
- **Current Observation**: Most existing solutions either cater exclusively to large health systems (Epic/MyChart) or lack patient-centric multi-provider portability.

### 2. Knowledge Base & Project Brain Setup
- **Focus**: Structuring the Obsidian vault into atomic concepts, requirements, and risk registries so architectural decisions are well-grounded.
- **Related Notes**: [[Development MOC]], [[MedRec MOC]], [[Decision Log]]

### 3. Early User Research Planning

## 📌 Context & Hypotheses

> [!NOTE]
> **Working Hypothesis**: Patients experience significant friction managing health records across independent clinics because no consumer-facing, standardized portable record exists that does not rely on direct institutional integration.
> 
> *Assumption*: Independent clinics are willing to accept structured patient-provided digital exports or summaries if formatted properly. (To be tested during user research).
