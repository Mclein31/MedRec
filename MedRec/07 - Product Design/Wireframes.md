## 🛠️ Tooling & Design Guidelines
- **Tooling**: Excalidraw, Figma, or Paper sketches.
- **Wireframe Principles**:
  - Focus purely on layout, visual hierarchy, spacing, and CTA prominence before introducing high-fidelity colors.
  - Test tap target sizes (minimum 44x44 pt on mobile).
  - Explicitly map empty states, error banners, and loading skeleton states.
