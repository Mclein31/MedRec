# User Personas

To prevent designing for hypothetical or imaginary users, personas in MedRec will **not** be invented from fiction. Instead, personas will be constructed and synthesized strictly from empirical research findings, user interviews, and observations gathered during [[Qualitative Research]].

This document defines the standard persona structure to be populated once field data is collected.

## Persona Specification Template

Each persona archetype derived from research must follow this structure:

```markdown
### [Archetype / Persona Name]

- **Role / Segment**: (e.g., Chronic Condition Patient, Primary Care Doctor, Caregiver)
- **Background**: Demographic summary, lifestyle context, healthcare encounter frequency.
- **Goals**: What are they trying to accomplish regarding their health and medical records?
  - Primary goal
  - Secondary goal
- **Frustrations**: Key obstacles, friction points, and pain points currently experienced.
  - Frustration 1
  - Frustration 2
- **Tech Comfort & Devices**: Comfort level with mobile apps, cloud services, and security tools (e.g., biometric login, password managers).
- **Representative Scenario**: A concrete walkthrough of a situation where they need to access, organize, or share medical records.
- **Validation Source**: Research session IDs or interview references supporting this persona.
```

## Target Segments Under Investigation

Based on initial domain mapping, research will target key groups across both [[Patients]] and [[Doctors]]:

1. **Chronic Illness Manager**: Patients who regularly visit multiple specialists and accumulate complex ongoing lab/medication histories.
2. **Family Caregiver**: Individuals managing health records on behalf of children, aging parents, or dependents.
3. **Transient / Relocating Patient**: Individuals switching healthcare networks due to relocation, new insurance, or changing providers.
4. **Primary Care Clinician**: Doctors needing quick, reliable intake data to understand a new patient's past trajectory.
