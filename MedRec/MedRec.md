# MedRec

**MedRec** is my personal project exploring how to solve healthcare data fragmentation by building a patient-centric medical records management and portability application. The core premise is simple: patients should own their health data, have immediate access to it, and be able to securely transfer or share it across clinics, specialists, and healthcare systems without friction.
