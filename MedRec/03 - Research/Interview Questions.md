# Interview Questions (Patient Research Guide)

> [!WARNING]
> **Status:** `DRAFT` — This guide contains preliminary interview prompts designed for semi-structured qualitative discovery with patients and family caregivers. Iterate on these questions as early interviews reveal unexpected problem domains.

---

## 🎙️ Interview Protocol & Guidelines

- **Estimated Time:** 35–45 minutes
- **Structure:** Semi-structured (allow the participant to expand on unexpected stories; probe deeper on moments of friction).
- **Researcher Mindset:** Listen without pitching solutions. Focus on past behavior ("Tell me about the last time you...") rather than speculative preference ("Would you like a feature that...").
- **Consent & Anonymity:** Remind the participant that no identifiable medical details will be recorded, and notes will be completely de-identified in [[Interview Notes]].

---

## 🧭 Question Guide by Topic

### 1. Warm-Up & Health Management Background (5 mins)
- *Goal: Understand participant context and health engagement intensity.*
1. Could you tell me a little about how often you or your family members interact with the healthcare system (e.g., primary care, specialists, hospitals)?
2. Roughly how many different doctors, clinics, or healthcare systems have you visited in the past 2–3 years?
3. How would you describe your general role in managing medical records for yourself or family members?

---

### 2. Current Record Management Experience (10 mins)
- *Goal: Uncover the current artifacts, tools, habits, and mental models.*
4. When you leave a doctor's appointment or complete a medical test, what happens to the paperwork, instructions, or test results you receive?
5. Where do your medical records currently live? (e.g., paper folders, filing cabinet, email attachments, digital portals, downloads on computer, mobile photos)?
6. How many different patient portals (e.g., MyChart, FollowMyHealth, clinic-specific portals) do you currently have logins for?
7. When you need to find a specific medical result from 1–2 years ago (like an MRI report, vaccination record, or blood panel), walk me through how you look for it. How long does that typically take?

---

### 3. Record Transfer & Sharing Experiences (10 mins)
- *Goal: Map the friction in cross-provider handoffs and transitions of care.*
8. Think back to the last time you visited a new doctor, specialist, or clinic. How did they get your past medical history?
9. Have you ever had to request records from one provider and send them to another? Walk me step-by-step through what that process was like.
10. Did you encounter any delays, fees, physical paperwork, or faxing requirements during that transfer?
11. Have you ever arrived at an appointment only to find out that the doctor did not have your previous records or test results? What happened as a result?

---

### 4. Pain Points & Unmet Needs (10 mins)
- *Goal: Expose emotional strain, administrative burden, and cognitive overload.*
12. What is the most frustrating or annoying part of managing your medical records today?
13. Have you ever had to repeat a medical test (e.g., blood draw, X-ray, scan) simply because the previous results couldn't be found or transferred in time?
14. If you had to explain your complete health history to a new doctor in 5 minutes, how confident would you feel doing so accurately from memory alone?
15. For caregivers: How do you handle keeping records organized across multiple dependents or family members?

---

### 5. Desired Solutions & Privacy Considerations (10 mins)
- *Goal: Test attitudes toward local-first data ownership vs cloud solutions.*
16. In an ideal world, what would your medical record management experience look like?
17. How do you feel about storing your complete medical history on a personal device (like your smartphone or laptop) versus a centralized health cloud service?
18. What would make you feel completely confident that your medical data is private and secure?
19. When sharing your records with a doctor, what format would be easiest for you to hand over (e.g., a PDF summary, a temporary secure link, a physical printout, an app)?

---

### 6. Wrap-Up & Closing (3 mins)
20. Is there anything else about managing or sharing your medical records that we haven't touched on today?
21. May I follow up with you in the future if I have questions or want to show you early prototypes?
