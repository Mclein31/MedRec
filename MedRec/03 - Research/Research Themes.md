## 🧭 Thematic Analysis Methodology

Thematic analysis in MedRec follows a bottom-up inductive coding workflow:

```
[ Raw Observations (⚪) in Interview Notes ]
                    │
                    ▼
       [ Open Coding & Affinity Grouping ]
                    │
                    ▼
        [ Emergent Research Themes ]
                    │
                    ▼
      [ Derived Strategic Insights (🔵) ]
```

1. **Extraction:** Capture atomic observations (quotes, pain points, behavioral quirks) from raw data.
2. **Affinity Mapping:** Group related observations into semantic clusters.
3. **Refinement:** Formalize recurring clusters into named, well-defined Themes with clear boundaries.
4. **Translation:** Connect themes directly to [[Research Insights]] and product strategy.

## 🎯 Candidate Thematic Buckets (Hypothesized to Watch)

The following candidate theme areas are hypothesized based on preliminary problem space exploration and will be evaluated during empirical coding:

### 1. *Portal Fragmentation & Login Fatigue*
- **Hypothesized Scope:** The cognitive and administrative burden of maintaining multiple disconnected patient portal accounts across health networks.
- **Related Hypothesis:** [[Research Hypotheses#H1 - Transfer Frustration|H1]]
- **Evidence Count:** `0 (Pending)`

### 2. *The Analogue Last-Mile Problem*
- **Hypothesized Scope:** Why and how physical paper, fax, and CD-ROMs persist as the de facto bridge between disparate EHR systems.
- **Related Hypothesis:** [[Research Hypotheses#H2 - Physical & Manual Dependency|H2]]
- **Evidence Count:** `0 (Pending)`

### 3. *Privacy vs. Convenience Paradox*
- **Hypothesized Scope:** Patient tension between wanting seamless instant data access while harboring deep mistrust toward commercial health data monetization.
- **Related Hypothesis:** [[Research Hypotheses#H4 - Privacy and Autonomy Concerns|H4]]
- **Evidence Count:** `0 (Pending)`

### 4. *Caregiver Proxy Burden*
- **Hypothesized Scope:** Inadequate software support for individuals acting as medical proxies for pediatric, aging, or multi-dependent family members.
- **Related Hypothesis:** [[Research Hypotheses#H1 - Transfer Frustration|H1]]
- **Evidence Count:** `0 (Pending)`
