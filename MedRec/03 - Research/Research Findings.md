---

## 🏷️ Epistemic Classification System

To maintain absolute clarity between assumptions, raw data, and proven facts, all knowledge artifacts in MedRec use this 5-tier epistemic taxonomy:

```
🔴 Assumption  ───(Formalize)───►  🟡 Hypothesis  ───(Empirical Test)───►
                                                                        │
⚪ Observation ───(Synthesize)───►  🟢 Finding    ───(Deduce)──────────► 🔵 Conclusion
```

### 1. 🔴 Assumption
- **Definition:** An unverified belief, intuitive premise, or working assumption taken for granted without empirical testing.
- **Rule:** Assumptions must NEVER be treated as requirements. They must be logged as hypotheses and subjected to testing.

### 2. 🟡 Hypothesis
- **Definition:** A falsifiable, testable proposition formulated to explain an observed friction or test a product premise.
- **Location:** Tracked in [[Research Hypotheses]].

### 3. ⚪ Observation
- **Definition:** A raw, direct data point, verbatim user quote, or specific behavior noted during research, without theoretical interpretation.
- **Location:** Recorded in [[Interview Notes]] and [[Literature Review]].

### 4. 🟢 Finding
- **Definition:** A validated, empirical pattern or truth supported by multiple convergent observations or peer-reviewed data.
- **Location:** Logged in this file with supporting evidence citations.

### 5. 🔵 Conclusion
- **Definition:** A high-level deduction, strategic insight, or architectural decision derived from multiple validated findings.
- **Location:** Synthesized in [[Research Insights]] and [[Decision Log|Architecture Decisions]].

## 🔬 Finding Entry Format Template

When adding a validated finding, use the following structure:

```markdown
### 🟢 [F-xx]: [Short, Descriptive Title of Finding]
- **Summary Statement:** A clear, one-sentence statement of the verified truth.
- **Epistemic Status:** 🟢 `VALIDATED FINDING`
- **Evidence Base:**
  - Observed in $X$ of $Y$ interviews in [[Interview Notes]] (`P01`, `P03`).
  - Supported by literature citation in [[Literature Review]] (`Smith et al., 2024`).
- **Context & Nuance:** Specific details on when this finding holds true and boundary conditions.
- **Impact on Product:**
  - Links to [[Research Themes]]
  - Generates [[Research Insights#INS-xx|Insight INS-xx]]
  - Informs [[Functional Requirements]]
```
