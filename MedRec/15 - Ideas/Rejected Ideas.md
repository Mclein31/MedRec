# Rejected Ideas

This document maintains an intentional registry of ideas, feature proposals, and technical architectures that were formally evaluated and rejected for [[MedRec]].

---

## 🛡️ Why Documenting Rejections Is Critical

In a solo software project, memory fades quickly. Six months from now, a tempting but deeply flawed idea will seem fresh and appealing again.

Documenting rejected ideas serves three key purposes:
1. **Prevents Circular Re-litigation**: Stops me from spending cycles exploring the exact same technical dead ends repeatedly.
2. **Defines Project Boundaries**: Saying "no" clearly sharpens what MedRec is—and more importantly, what it is *not*.
3. **Preserves Context**: Documents the trade-offs and constraints (regulatory, architectural, cognitive) that led to the dismissal.

---

## 📝 Rejection Template

When an idea from [[Future Ideas]] or [[Experimental Ideas]] is dismissed, record it using this standard structure:

```markdown
### [Idea Name / Concept Title]
- **Date Rejected**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Initial Concept / Why Considered**: Brief summary of what the idea was and what problem it aimed to solve.
- **Why Rejected**: The critical failure mode, fatal flaw, disproportionate complexity, or philosophical misalignment.
- **Key Assumptions Disproven**: What belief turned out to be false.
- **Alternative Adopted**: What approach or decision was chosen instead.
- **Related Notes**: [[Relevant Note]]
```
